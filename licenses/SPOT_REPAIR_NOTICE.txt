PortraitLab adaptation in portraitlab/spot_repair.py:
- Explicit normalised target and donor points instead of a lasso and offset.
- Target low-frequency tone from surrounding pixels; real donor texture ratio
  in linear light, following the upstream Patch approach.
- Strict finite circular support with inward feather, exact unchanged exterior.
- Immutable input donor samples and sequential target compositing for replay.
- Bilinear subpixel donor sampling; out-of-image donor pixels are not applied.
- Bounded tone thumbnails, tiled full-resolution texture, parameter validation,
  zero-strength/same-source identity, no-op and edge diagnostics.
- Stabilised texture ratio for very dark/black donor pixels.

This is an adapted local healing algorithm, not an OpenCV inpainting model,
not an automatic blemish detector and not generative facial reconstruction.
User selection determines which local features are replaced. Quality depends
on suitable donor texture and target selection; no identity-preservation or
general image-quality superiority claim is made by this integration.
